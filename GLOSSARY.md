# Glossary

**Challenge Fruit**: A rare fruit that appears on the board for a short time at regular intervals. Eating it starts a Typing Challenge; it gives no score and does not grow the snake.
_Avoid_: special fruit, bonus fruit

**Typing Challenge**: A timed event, started by eating a Challenge Fruit, where the snake slows down and the player must type a shown game-themed word (4–6 letters) before time runs out. Succeeding earns a Life.
_Avoid_: QTE, quick time event, typing test

**Life**: A spare chance the player holds (at most 3), earned only from a Typing Challenge. Hitting a wall or the snake's own body while holding a Life spends it instead of ending the game.
_Avoid_: heart, extra life

**Invulnerability**: A short period after spending a Life during which the snake cannot die.
_Avoid_: immortality, god mode

**Wall Event**: The recurring event where the board walls warn, then turn solid (deadly) until the player scores enough to open them again.
